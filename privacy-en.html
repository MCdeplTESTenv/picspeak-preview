Privacy

PicSpeak collects no data. There are no accounts, no PicSpeak servers, no ads and no analytics.

Cookies and data on the device

  • PicSpeak uses no cookies: no profiling, analytics or third-party cookies. There are no pixels, counters or scripts from other sites.
  • Boards, photos, usage habits, settings and the PIN fingerprint (never the PIN itself) stay on the device, in the browser’s storage (IndexedDB). A copy of the tool’s files and of the downloaded symbol packs lets it work without internet. After a reload, a session note lasting a few moments reopens the same page.
  • This data is only used to make PicSpeak work and nobody receives it: that is why no consent banner is needed. It is deleted with Delete everything in the settings or by clearing the site’s data in the browser.

External services

As with any website, whoever answers a request sees the IP address and the browser type. PicSpeak only contacts:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the site: when PicSpeak is opened, an update arrives or a symbol pack is downloaded.
  • ARASAAC (arasaac.org), when searching symbols online: it only receives the searched word and sends the images.
  • MyMemory (mymemory.translated.net), only when the word must be translated to English to search a symbol: it only receives that word.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net), only with Twemoji emoji turned on: the search runs on the device, jsDelivr does not receive the word but sees which emoji are shown.
  • With online search off (Settings › General › Internet and data usage) these search services are never contacted.
  • The voices are the device’s own. Voices marked “internet only” belong to the maker of the browser or system (for example Google, Microsoft, Apple), which receives the text to read: to avoid this, choose a voice without that label.

Online backup (Dropbox)

  • If the adult connects the online backup, PicSpeak saves a copy only in the Apps/PicSpeak folder of their Dropbox, and only for the profiles they turn it on for. The copy goes directly from the device to Dropbox: nobody else receives it. PicSpeak cannot see or touch the other files in the Dropbox. Each profile has a folder named after the profile, chosen by the adult.
  • PicSpeak does not ask Dropbox for the account’s name or email: it only uses its own folder.
  • The Dropbox permission stays on this device only. With Disconnect PicSpeak gives it back to Dropbox; the copies already saved stay in the adult’s Dropbox, and they can delete them whenever they like.
  • The copies on Dropbox have no password of their own: the Dropbox account protects them. Use a strong password and turn on Dropbox two-step verification.
