Privacy

PicSpeak collects no data. There are no accounts, no PicSpeak servers, no ads and no analytics.

  • Boards, photos, usage habits and settings stay on the device, in the browser.
  • If the adult connects the online backup, PicSpeak saves a copy only in the Apps/PicSpeak folder of their Dropbox, and only for the profiles they turn it on for. The copy goes directly from the device to Dropbox: nobody else receives it. PicSpeak cannot see or touch the other files in the Dropbox. Each profile has a folder named after the profile, chosen by the adult.
  • PicSpeak does not ask Dropbox for the account’s name or email: it only uses its own folder.
  • The Dropbox permission stays on this device only. With Disconnect PicSpeak gives it back to Dropbox; the copies already saved stay in the adult’s Dropbox, and they can delete them whenever they like.
  • The copies on Dropbox have no password of their own: the Dropbox account protects them. Use a strong password and turn on Dropbox two-step verification.
  • When searching for new symbols online, only the searched word (or the emoji code) leaves the device.
