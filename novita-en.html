What’s new

Every version of PicSpeak has a number in two parts, for example v1.0: the first number goes up with major changes, the second with smaller improvements. PicSpeak updates itself when the device is connected to the internet.

v1.0 · November 2026

The first public version.

  • Communicating: a board of tiles with pictures and words, sentence read aloud, folders, quick buttons, a bigger sentence to show.
  • Preparing boards: ready-made boards, ARASAAC symbols and other sources, personal photos, several tiles at once, colours by word type, lockable positions, printing.
  • For each person: several profiles on the same device, each with its own boards and choices; ready-made styles and adjustable look.
  • Prediction and contexts: words suggested from use, time of day and contexts (for example “School”), with the privacy PIN for the child’s habits.
  • Protection: settings protected by a sum or a PIN, the “Getting started” tutorial and a guide for adults.
  • Data kept safe: backup to a file, with an optional password, and online backup on Dropbox. Every backup remembers which version created it.
  • Sharing: templates to exchange with other adults, and boards from and to other apps (Open Board).
  • App: installs on the Home screen and works without internet.
